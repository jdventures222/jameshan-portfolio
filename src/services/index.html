FAQ

Who owns what you build?

You do: the domain, the code and the accounts. Everything runs on your accounts, and I hand over every login.

How much does it cost?

You get a fixed price in writing within a few days of our first talk.

What if I stop care?

Everything keeps running on your accounts. Later changes are quoted one at a time.

Does it work with my POS?

Restaurant websites link to the online ordering Square, Toast and Clover already include. For loyalty, gift cards, texting or other work inside your POS, tell me which system you use and what you want it to do. I’ll confirm what’s possible before we start.

How is this different from a restaurant platform?

I build what your restaurant needs, and it runs on accounts you own. You talk to me, not a team. If you want a full marketing suite, a platform may suit you better.
