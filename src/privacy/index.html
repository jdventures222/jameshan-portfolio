Privacy

This site sets no cookies, and its pages load nothing from any other site.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs each visitor’s IP address for security.

The request form asks for your name, your email and what you want built, and, if you like, what kind of project it is and when you need it. Your request goes through Formspark, which emails it to me. Accessibility

I aim for this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

I check every page with an automated test in Chrome, at phone and desktop sizes, in light and dark mode, and with JavaScript on and off. It checks that:

  • Every link, button and form field can be reached with the Tab key and shows a visible focus ring.
  • Text stands out from its background by at least the contrast WCAG AA asks for.
  • Links, buttons and form fields are at least 44 pixels tall, and every field has a label.
  • Animations and transitions stop when your device asks for less motion.
  • No page scrolls sideways at five widths from 320 to 1440 pixels.
